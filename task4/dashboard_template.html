Comparison details

Click a column heading to sort. Change is calculated between the selected start and end years.

Method: GDP in billions of constant 2020 USD; GHG in million tonnes CO₂e excluding LULUCF. Intensity = summed GHG (kt) / summed GDP (USD bn). Regional values are sums of the selected countries, not full regional inventories. The regional classification follows exactly the nine UN M49-style subregions in Task 3’s analysis.py. Regional intensity is a ratio of sums, not an average of national intensities.

Decoupling rule: Task 3 uses six sign-based categories: absolute, relative, coupled, emissions growth exceeds GDP growth, GDP non-growth with falling emissions, and GDP non-growth with non-falling emissions. Single-year endpoint comparisons are not available. Elasticity = emissions change / GDP change; undefined for near-zero GDP change. No causal inference.

Quality: Task 3 requires all 43 × 31 country-year keys, finite positive values and no duplicates. Invalid workbooks are rejected, not silently imputed. Source workbook notes report 43 matched countries, 1,333 records and no missing GDP/GHG. No worldwide inference is implied.

Provenance: analysis.py · QJ8W7I-task-3. Python-built mode embeds its generated tables. Workbook mode applies the same formulas in the browser; it does not execute Python.
